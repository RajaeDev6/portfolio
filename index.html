Shop Weh

Grocery price comparison for Jamaica · Live

Add your grocery list and Shop Weh ranks Jamaican stores by how much of the list they carry and what it costs, then shows the cheapest store and how much you save compared with the average. Installable as a PWA.

  • 4,800+ real prices from 86 stores across all 11 parishes
  • Scrapers for Consumer Affairs Commission survey PDFs, WooCommerce and Shopify feeds that obey robots.txt
  • Daily price refresh with GitHub Actions, published to Firestore
  • User accounts, price checks and reports with Firebase Auth

Chichi Bus

Crowdsourced JUTC bus tracker · Live

JUTC doesn’t publish live bus locations, so riders tell each other. Ask riders on your route, report “I see a bus”, and the app moves an estimated bus along the real road toward you with a live ETA.

  • All 159 JUTC routes and 208 stops, scraped from the official route guide
  • Routes snapped to real roads with OSRM, using a dynamic-programming pass to avoid U-turn loops
  • Location is only sent when you confirm; live sharing is a separate opt-in
  • Offline-ready PWA with precached route data
Preact Firebase Leaflet OpenStreetMap Vite
