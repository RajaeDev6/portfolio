Chichi Bus

Crowdsourced JUTC bus tracker · Live

JUTC doesn’t publish live bus locations, so riders tell each other. Ask riders on your route, report “I see a bus”, and the app moves an estimated bus along the real road toward you with a live ETA.

  • All 159 JUTC routes and 208 stops, scraped from the official route guide
  • Routes snapped to real roads with OSRM, using a dynamic-programming pass to avoid U-turn loops
  • Location is only sent when you confirm; live sharing is a separate opt-in
  • Offline-ready PWA with precached route data
